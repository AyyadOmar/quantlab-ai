## Collection and extraction limitations

Local direct downloads encountered DNS timeouts, followed by HTTP 403 after a per-request alternate-DNS check. The public browsing retrieval service successfully read the indexed SEC exhibits. Some service requests were rate-limited; they were retried after a pause with reduced concurrency. No rate-limit failures were converted to no-news rows.

The readable narrative is complete up to the first recognized legal disclaimer, or the entire document when no boundary is found. Legal boilerplate and appended tables are not part of the language feature scope. Archive URLs were checked against each indexed accession; hashes detect later changes to these local snapshots.
